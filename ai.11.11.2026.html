Model releases & capability updates

Anthropic
Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 released, with a lower Fable price [source]
Anthropic released both models on Nov 9. Fable 5.2 is generally available on the Claude Platform, Claude Code, AWS, Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure, and its price falls to $8/$40 per million input/output tokens (from $10 input). Anthropic claims up to 30% faster output and about 55% fewer blocked requests than Fable 5.1. Mythos 5.2 stays restricted to vetted organizations through Anthropic's Life Sciences and Cyber Verification programs, and its system card flags stronger covert capabilities than earlier models.
Why it matters: Fable 5.2 is available through Azure and Microsoft Foundry, so Claude-based Copilot Studio or Foundry agents can switch to it, and the price cut lowers per-call costs for Dynamics 365 automation workloads.
Google
Gemini 4 Carbon rolls out in the Gemini app for AI Ultra subscribers [source]
Per 9to5Google (Nov 10), Carbon, Google's top Gemini 4 model, is reaching the Gemini app for AI Ultra subscribers, while Gemini 4 Argon remains the default for AI Pro and above. Carbon was announced Oct 28 with limited early access. Its introductory API price matches Argon's at $2/$10 per million input/output tokens. Argon's introductory pricing ends Nov 30, after which it rises to $4/$20.
Why it matters: If you're comparing Gemini against Azure OpenAI or Claude for agent backends, budget at the post-Nov 30 rates, not the introductory ones.

Pricing / API / availability changes

Microsoft
Claude to be switched on by default for UK Microsoft 365 Copilot tenants on Dec 9 [source]
Message Center item MC1546435 (reported Nov 10) says UK tenants that have not set the Anthropic subprocessor option will have Claude models enabled on Dec 9, matching other regions. Tenants that have already chosen a setting keep it. The limited 'Copilot in Microsoft 365 apps only' option is being retired, and tenants using it move to All users. Anthropic processes this data in the US, outside Microsoft's EU Data Boundary.
Why it matters: This needs an admin decision before Dec 9. UK partners and clients should review data-residency obligations and set the toggle explicitly rather than accept the default.
OpenAI
ChatGPT Enterprise now sold through AWS Marketplace [source]
Since Nov 9, AWS customers can buy ChatGPT Enterprise through AWS Marketplace on private-offer pricing, with spend counting toward their AWS EDP or PPA commitments. OpenAI says it has the same models and features as buying direct. ChatGPT for Healthcare and ChatGPT for Financial Services are also listed for eligible customers.
Why it matters: This makes ChatGPT Enterprise easier to buy for AWS-committed customers, which matters in head-to-head deals against Microsoft 365 Copilot.

Product & platform launches

OpenAI
'Businesses in ChatGPT': Business Hub profiles and customer-facing Business Agents (beta) [source]
Launched Nov 10. Business Hub lets verified companies manage a profile in ChatGPT and see basic insights such as views, mentions and clicks. Eligible US e-commerce and online-service businesses can also deploy a Business Agent that answers questions about their products. Agents can be promoted through labelled sponsored placements, and OpenAI says businesses do not receive users' conversations.
Why it matters: ChatGPT is becoming a customer-engagement channel that overlaps with Dynamics 365 Customer Service and Copilot Studio customer agents. Expect clients to ask how the two fit together.
