Jul 22 – Jul 26, 2026

Model releases & capability updates

Anthropic
Claude Opus 5 launches, closing in on flagship performance at half the price [source]
Anthropic released Claude Opus 5, its strongest Opus-tier model yet, with gains in agentic coding, computer use, and long-horizon knowledge work, plus a new low/medium/high effort control. It's priced the same as Opus 4.8 ($5/$25 per million tokens) while approaching Claude Fable 5's frontier performance, and becomes the default model for Claude Max subscribers.
Why it matters: Opus 5 shipped simultaneously on Microsoft Foundry (alongside AWS and Google Cloud), giving Dynamics 365/Power Platform partners a cheaper, near-frontier Claude option to plug into Copilot Studio and Foundry Agent Service workflows without waiting for a separate Azure rollout.

Pricing / API / availability changes

xAI
Grok STT 1.0 speech-to-text API released [source]
xAI shipped Grok STT 1.0, a speech-to-text API with word-level timestamps, speaker diarization, and multichannel audio support, priced at $0.10/hour. It's also now accessible through OpenRouter, widening access beyond a direct xAI API account.
Why it matters: A cheap, dedicated transcription API is a plug-in option for partners building voice-enabled Power Platform apps who want an alternative to Azure Speech, though it remains a second-tier vendor with no direct Microsoft integration yet.

Product & platform launches

OpenAI
Presence: a managed platform for enterprise voice/chat agent deployment [source]
OpenAI launched Presence, an enterprise offering for deploying customer-facing and internal voice/chat agents that can access company systems, take approved actions, and escalate to humans under org-defined policies. It's rolling out via limited GA with OpenAI's Forward Deployed Engineers and systems integrators rather than self-service; OpenAI says the approach resolves 75% of its own support inquiries without a human.
Why it matters: Presence competes directly with the managed-agent-deployment model Microsoft is building around Foundry Agent Service and Copilot Studio — a sign that white-glove enterprise agent rollouts, not just APIs, are becoming the next competitive front D365 partners should watch.
OpenAI
ChatGPT Health rolls out to all US users [source]
OpenAI expanded ChatGPT Health, letting logged-in US users connect health records and Apple Health data for a personal health dashboard and grounded Q&A on labs, medications, and wellness trends, on web and iOS.
Why it matters: Limited direct Dynamics 365/Power Platform relevance, but it shows OpenAI continuing to build vertical, data-grounded consumer experiences on top of ChatGPT — a pattern that mirrors how Copilot is extending into vertical, data-grounded scenarios.

Partnerships, funding, acquisitions

Anthropic
AMD to invest up to $5B in Anthropic in chips-for-equity compute deal [source]
AMD and Anthropic announced a strategic partnership under which Anthropic will deploy up to two gigawatts of AMD Instinct MI450-series GPUs starting in H1 2027, with AMD investing up to $5 billion in Anthropic contingent on deployment milestones.
Why it matters: Diversifies Anthropic's compute supply beyond Nvidia, which should help stabilize capacity and pricing for Claude models used in enterprise and Azure Foundry deployments over time.
Microsoft
Microsoft and Databricks extend strategic partnership into the 2030s [source]
Microsoft and Databricks expanded their decade-long partnership, with Databricks deepening its use of Azure (including Azure Cobalt Arm infrastructure) and Microsoft integrating Databricks' Genie AI co-worker and Unity Catalog data grounding more directly into its own products.
Why it matters: Deeper Databricks-Azure integration means more enterprise data/AI context becoming available to ground Copilot and Power Platform apps in customers' own business data — worth tracking for D365 implementations that also run Databricks.
