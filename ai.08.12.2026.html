Aug 9 – Aug 12, 2026

Model releases & capability updates

OpenAI
OpenAI unveils "Doug," its largest pre-training run in two years [source]
On August 9, OpenAI revealed Doug, described as its largest foundation-model pre-training run to date, targeted for launch by November 2026. It marks a return to base-model scaling after roughly two years in which OpenAI's gains came mainly from reinforcement learning and inference-time compute rather than bigger pretraining runs; Doug is separate from the delayed Astra/GPT-6 line.
Why it matters: A renewed scaling push from OpenAI raises the ceiling on the underlying models behind Microsoft 365 Copilot and other Azure AI Foundry offerings that run OpenAI models as a subprocessor.
OpenAI
OpenAI launches GPT-5.6-Cyber, splits Daybreak into Blue/Red tiers [source]
On August 10, OpenAI released GPT-5.6-Cyber, a cybersecurity-specialized variant of its GPT-5.6 Sol flagship, available only through the new Daybreak Red tier for vetted vulnerability researchers; a separate Daybreak Blue tier gives approved defenders unrestricted access to general-purpose models. OpenAI said the model completed 95% of advanced security test tasks and helped uncover two previously unknown Chrome V8 vulnerabilities, now patched as CVE-2026-15903.
Why it matters: Frontier labs are now productizing offensive/defensive security tooling directly into model lineups — worth tracking for partners handling regulated D365 data who may want AI-assisted security review, or who need to account for AI-accelerated attack tooling in risk assessments.

Pricing / API / availability changes

Mistral AI
Mistral makes Regional Endpoints GA, adds SLA-backed Priority Tier [source]
On August 11, Mistral AI announced Regional Endpoints are now generally available, letting customers pin inference to Europe or the US, plus a new Priority Tier (public preview) with committed rate limits and an uptime SLA. It also announced a multi-year European compute coalition — including Amadeus, ASML, and CMA CGM — to fund 200MW of infrastructure by 2027 and a full gigawatt by 2030.
Why it matters: Data-residency guarantees and SLA-backed tiers give EU-based Microsoft partners another sovereign-AI option to weigh against Azure OpenAI / Azure AI Foundry when data-locality requirements apply.

Product & platform launches

Anthropic
Anthropic makes Claude Code's autonomous "auto mode" the default [source]
Announced August 9: starting August 14, Claude Code will default to auto mode for Pro, Max, and Team accounts, proceeding through steps without per-action approval prompts unless a step is judged irreversible, destructive, or reaches outside the user's environment. Manual approval mode remains available as an option.
Why it matters: Directly affects how partners running Claude Code for D365/Power Platform development configure guardrails — teams should review auto-mode's irreversible-action boundaries before the August 14 default flip.
Google
Gemini passes 1 billion monthly active users [source]
Google said on August 11 that Gemini has surpassed 1 billion monthly active users, with more than 100 million of those on Apple devices — a milestone landing the same week ChatGPT crossed 1 billion weekly users.
Why it matters: Scale milestones like this shape which assistant ecosystem (Copilot, Gemini, or ChatGPT) ends up as the default AI surface end users expect your D365/Power Platform solutions to integrate with.
xAI
xAI opens public beta of Grok Bot, an always-on autonomous agent [source]
On August 11, xAI opened a public beta of Grok Bot, an agent that runs on its own persistent cloud computer and drives existing tools directly (mouse/keyboard-style, no API connectors needed), so tasks keep running after a laptop closes. Access is bundled into SuperGrok Heavy, Cursor Ultra, and Cursor Teams Premium on desktop and iOS, with a wider rollout tied to Grok 4.6 expected later the same week.
Why it matters: Another entrant in the always-on, computer-use agent race alongside Copilot Actions — worth monitoring for automation patterns that could inform Power Automate / Copilot Studio agent design.
