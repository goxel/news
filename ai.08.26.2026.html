AI Vendor Digest

Aug 23 – Aug 26, 2026

Model releases & capability updates

OpenAI
Jalapeño custom inference chip shows major speed and efficiency gains [source]
OpenAI published first benchmark results for Jalapeño, its in-house inference chip, showing 1.5–1.9x more AI work per watt and 1.7–3.6x lower end-to-end latency versus comparison systems across GPT-OSS 120B, DeepSeek R1, and Kimi K2.5. Deployment into OpenAI's own compute infrastructure is planned by year-end, with broader external availability in 2027.
Why it matters: Custom silicon aimed at lower latency/higher throughput could eventually translate into cheaper, faster Copilot/GPT-backed experiences in Dynamics 365 and Power Platform once the underlying models it serves reach Azure-hosted deployments.

Pricing / API / availability changes

OpenAI
GPT-5.6 Sol API pricing cut by over 20% for three months [source]
OpenAI reduced GPT-5.6 Sol API rates from $5/$30 to $4/$20 per million input/output tokens, a discount that also applies to token-based credits on ChatGPT Work and Codex. The reduced pricing runs for three months; subscription pricing is unaffected.
Why it matters: Lower GPT-5.6 Sol token costs directly reduce the cost of any partner-built Copilot Studio or Power Platform solution that calls OpenAI models via Azure AI Foundry or the OpenAI API.
OpenAI
5-hour usage cap reinstated for Codex and ChatGPT Work on Plus plans [source]
OpenAI announced on Aug 24 that it is restoring the rolling 5-hour usage window for Codex and ChatGPT Work on standard Plus subscriptions, effective Aug 25, after six weeks of uncapped access. Pro-tier ($100/$200) subscriptions remain exempt from the cap.
Why it matters: Partners piloting Codex or ChatGPT Work for internal dev/support workflows alongside Dynamics 365 projects should expect throttling on Plus-tier seats again and budget Pro seats for heavy users.
OpenAI
o3 retired from ChatGPT [source]
OpenAI retired the o3 reasoning model from ChatGPT on Aug 26, closing a 90-day sunset window that began in late May. The change applies only to ChatGPT; o3 remains available via the API until its separate December 2026 shutdown.
Why it matters: Low direct impact on D365/Power Platform integrations (which typically call the API, not ChatGPT), but signals OpenAI's continued consolidation toward the GPT-5.6 family.

Product & platform launches

Microsoft
Dynamics 365, Power Platform, and Dataverse roadmaps merge into the AI at Work roadmap [source]
Starting in September 2026, Microsoft is folding Dynamics 365, Power Platform, and Dataverse roadmap content into the unified Microsoft 365 "AI at Work" roadmap, giving customers and partners one place to track upcoming capabilities across Dynamics 365, Power Platform, Microsoft 365, Copilot, and Copilot Studio agents.
Why it matters: Direct relevance for Microsoft Partners: this changes where D365/Power Platform partners should track feature rollout and plan customer adoption, consolidating what were previously separate release-plan sites.
OpenAI
Admin plugin launches for ChatGPT Work and Codex [source]
OpenAI released an Admin plugin that lets workspace admins review usage, manage members and groups, adjust access/permissions, and approve spending requests for ChatGPT Work and Codex through conversational commands instead of a separate admin console.
Why it matters: A preview of the conversational-admin pattern Microsoft is pushing for Copilot management (e.g., the Purview AI Action Ledger) — useful reference point for partners advising customers on governance of AI seats.
Anthropic
Claude Tag in Slack now reads full channel context before responding [source]
Anthropic removed the per-message classifier that previously decided in isolation whether Claude Tag should respond in Slack, replacing it with full-conversation context plus memory and standing instructions. Anthropic reports the change makes Claude roughly 30% better at judging when to jump into a conversation unprompted.
Why it matters: Illustrates the "ambient agent" pattern (an assistant that reads full context and decides whether to act) that Copilot Studio agents in Teams/Dynamics 365 are also moving toward — worth watching as a UX benchmark.
