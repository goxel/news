Pricing / API / availability changes

Google
Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash: 15-second video clips at ~30% lower price [source]
Google made Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash generally available on Nov 5, with native clips up to 15 seconds (3–15s in 1-second steps). Reported 720p pricing is about $0.07 per second, roughly 30% below Omni 1.1. Secondary coverage notes the public API pricing page lagged at the time.
Why it matters: Cheaper generative video lowers the cost of embedding media generation in customer-facing apps; verify pricing on Google's own docs before budgeting.

Product & platform launches

Anthropic
Personal Finance in Claude launches in US beta [source]
On Nov 5 Anthropic began rolling out Personal Finance for US Pro, Max and Ultra subscribers. Users link bank and card accounts via Plaid for read-only analysis of spending, bills and balances. Anthropic says chats are not used for training and there are no ads or sponsored recommendations.
Why it matters: Shows the pattern of read-only, connector-based data access in assistants, a useful reference for governance design when connecting Copilot/agents to Dynamics 365 or Dataverse data.
OpenAI
Images 2.6 adds animated GIF creation to ChatGPT [source]
Launched Nov 4 across ChatGPT and Codex on all plans: GIFs from prompts or uploaded photos, with speed and frame-level editing, and reportedly 50% faster generation. Two API models, Flare and Sunburst, were also introduced; GIF rollout is gradual.
Why it matters: Mostly a consumer and creative feature, with limited direct Dynamics 365 / Power Platform impact.
