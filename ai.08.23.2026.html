OpenAI cuts GPT-5.6 Sol API pricing by more than 20% for three months [source]
On August 21, OpenAI dropped GPT-5.6 Sol short-context API pricing from $5/$30 to $4/$20 per million input/output tokens, promotional through at least November 21, 2026. The cut also applies to eligible ChatGPT Work and Codex credit plans, while Pro, Plus, and Business subscription prices are unchanged. It is OpenAI's second pricing move in under a month, following late-July cuts to the cheaper Terra and Luna tiers, as it responds to competition from Anthropic and Chinese model providers.
Why it matters: Azure OpenAI Service has committed to pricing parity with OpenAI's API, so partners running Copilot Studio agents or Azure AI Foundry workloads on GPT-5.6 Sol should see matching Azure cost reductions — worth revisiting TCO estimates for Sol-backed Dynamics 365 / Power Platform solutions.
