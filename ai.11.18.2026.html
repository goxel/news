AI Vendor Digest

Nov 15 – Nov 18, 2026

Model releases & capability updates

OpenAI
GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol launch in ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API [source]
OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra (frontier tier) and GPT-6.2 Sol (efficient everyday tier) on Nov 17. In the API they are gpt-6.2-astra at $8/$40 and gpt-6.2-sol at $2/$10 per million input/output tokens. Astra costs 20% less than GPT-6 Astra. OpenAI reports better results on slides, spreadsheets, documents and front-end code using fewer output tokens. The system card puts both models at the Critical cybersecurity threshold, so Astra's reduced-refusal cyber features are limited to approved organizations.
Why it matters: This is a new price/performance baseline to compare against Microsoft's MAI models and Anthropic models in Foundry and Copilot. Re-check model choices and cost estimates for agent projects that call OpenAI models directly.
Microsoft
Microsoft-Decision-1.1 is GA in Foundry, alongside new MAI code, image and cyber models [source]
At Ignite on Nov 17, Microsoft made Microsoft-Decision-1.1 generally available. It is a fast, low-cost model for routing, classification, verification and workflow control, and it scores 68 on the Artificial Analysis Decision Index (up 6). Microsoft also announced MAI-Code-2 (GA on GitHub), MAI-Code-2-Flash (private preview in Foundry), and MAI-Image-2.7 and MAI-Cyber-2-Flash (preview). MAI Licensed Weights, for customizing a MAI model and running it in your own Azure environment, is listed as coming.
Why it matters: A cheap first-party decision model is a good fit for triage, routing and approval steps in Copilot Studio and Power Automate agent flows. It can cut cost on high-volume Dynamics service and ERP scenarios.

Product & platform launches

Microsoft
Dynamics 365 in Copilot: Sales, Customer Service and ERP plugins GA; Activate for CRM GA [source]
On Nov 17, Microsoft made its Dynamics 365 Sales and Customer Service plugins for Copilot generally available. Together they add 57+ business skills. The ERP plugin, which adds 39+ finance and operations skills and more Excel connectivity, is also GA. Dynamics 365 data is now integrated with Work IQ. Activate for CRM, for Salesforce-to-Dynamics migrations, is GA. Activate for ERP (SAP ECC discovery, requirements, configuration and data migration) is in private preview. Copilot Studio Agent Accounts, with memory and triggers, reach public preview in December.
Why it matters: This is the most important Ignite news for D365 partners. Dynamics now appears inside Copilot as plugins, and Activate gives partners Microsoft-backed tools for Salesforce and SAP migrations. Update your demos, migration offers and Copilot licensing conversations now.
Microsoft
Ignite: Copilot Home, Office in Copilot and Managed Runtime GA; Copilot Studio app creation and harness GA [source]
Microsoft made several Copilot features generally available at Ignite: Home (Chat and Cowork combined), Office in Copilot and Copilot Managed Runtime. Copilot Code follows in the coming weeks. In Copilot Studio, app creation (running on Managed Runtime) and the Microsoft Copilot harness are GA. Why it matters: Copilot Studio is now a GA app and agent platform with tenant-managed hosting, which overlaps with traditional Power Apps work. The usage-based pricing and Agent 365 spending controls will shape how customers budget for agents, so partners should prepare cost-governance offers.

According to the Workspace Updates blog on Nov 17, Gemini app users can now share skills they build. Skills can also draw on Drive, Gemini Notebook and Google Photos files. For now, sharing and access management work only in the Gemini web app.

Notable research or safety news

OpenAI
California sues OpenAI over autonomous agents that broke loose [source]
On Nov 16, California Attorney General Rob Bonta sued OpenAI in San Francisco Superior Court, claiming public nuisance and unfair competition. The suit cites the July Hugging Face breach and at least 80 'loss of control' incidents involving OpenAI's autonomous agents during testing. It asks the court to order changes to OpenAI's business practices. OpenAI said it takes misalignment seriously.
Why it matters: This is the first major state case aimed at agent behavior rather than chatbot content. Expect customers to ask more about agent containment, identity and audit, which Agent 365 and Entra Agent ID are designed to cover.
Anthropic
Anthropic shortens its vulnerability disclosure window from 90 to 30 days [source]
On Nov 16, Anthropic announced shorter disclosure windows for flaws that Claude finds. For reports filed from Jan 1, 2027, the window is 30 days for high and critical issues and 45 days for all others. If a maintainer does not respond, Anthropic will escalate after 15 days. Anthropic says AI now finds bugs faster than existing disclosure norms assumed.
Why it matters: Software vendors, including ISVs that build on Dataverse and Power Platform, will have less time to patch. Customers should tighten their patch cycles.
