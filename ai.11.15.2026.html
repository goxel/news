AI Vendor Digest

Nov 11 – Nov 15, 2026

Model releases & capability updates

Google
Gemini 4 Hydrogen: a fast, cheaper Gemini 4 model for coding and agent work [source]
Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen on Nov 12. It is the fast tier beside Gemini 4 Carbon and Argon. Google says it beats Argon on the Artificial Analysis index, runs about 2.5× faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash and supports 1M-token input and output. It costs $1.50/$7.50 per million input/output tokens and is available in the Gemini API, AI Studio, the Gemini app, Search and Sheets.
Why it matters: It sets a new price/performance point for high-volume agent workloads. Use it as a benchmark when choosing models for Copilot Studio or Foundry agents in multi-model customer projects.
Alibaba / Qwen
Qwen4-Max enters limited preview [source]
Several trackers report that Alibaba opened a preview of Qwen4-Max on Nov 11, available only through its Token Plan, with the preview window closing Nov 25. Alibaba has not confirmed dates or open-weight plans for the rest of the Qwen 4 family; reports conflict.
Why it matters: Qwen open weights are often used for low-cost or self-hosted workloads. Wait for official specs and licensing before planning any production use.

Pricing / API / availability changes

Microsoft
Commercial Cloud local-currency prices change Jan 1, 2027 (INR +15.7%, NZD +10%) [source]
Microsoft told partners on Nov 11 that its annual local-currency adjustment takes effect Jan 1, 2027. Cloud prices go up 15.7% in INR and 10% in NZD, and down 4% in AUD, 5.9% in NOK and up to 8.5% in CNY.
Why it matters: This changes CSP quotes and renewals for Dynamics 365, Power Platform and Copilot in these markets. Lock in renewals or re-quote affected customers before January.
Microsoft
Microsoft Purview Data Governance is being retired (end of support Nov 12, 2027) [source]
On Nov 12, Microsoft announced that Purview Data Governance is being retired, including the Unified Catalog, Data Map, Workflows and Azure Purview. Support ends Nov 12, 2027.
Why it matters: Customers who catalog Dataverse or Fabric data for Copilot and agent grounding with Purview need a migration plan. This is a good reason to raise data-governance roadmaps with customers now.

Product & platform launches

Microsoft
Agent 365 module added to the Frontier Accelerate for Security partner kit [source]
On Nov 12, Microsoft added a trial-based Microsoft Agent 365 module to its Frontier Accelerate for Security Envisioning & POC kit. Partners can use it to run customer workshops on observing, governing and securing AI agents. Ignite runs Nov 17–20, so expect more agent announcements next week.
Why it matters: Governance of Copilot Studio and Dynamics agents is becoming a funded topic for partners. Agent 365 workshops are a way to start agent-security projects.
OpenAI
ChatGPT Wallet lets ChatGPT agents make purchases with tokenized cards [source]
OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet on Nov 13 for US ChatGPT Pro users. It was built with Visa, Mastercard and American Express. Agents pay with tokenized cards, so they never see the real card number, and every purchase needs a biometric approval. OpenAI also published a Commerce Partner Playbook for merchants.
Why it matters: This is an early standard for letting agents pay. Commerce and D365 Commerce teams should check that their fraud rules and checkout flows do not block legitimate agent orders.
